How FloodGuard BD works

Simple on the surface, detailed underneath.

📡

1. Collect

River observations and environmental inputs provide the raw signals.

🧹

2. Process

Recent levels, rate of change, reference thresholds and patterns are organized for analysis.

🤖

3. Predict

A machine-learning workflow estimates future water-level behaviour and risk signals.

🗣️

4. Explain

The interface converts complex signals into understandable summaries, maps and forecasts.
