How FloodGuard BD works

Simple explanation, deeper detail underneath
📡

1. Collect

River observations and related environmental inputs provide the raw signals.

🧹

2. Process

Recent levels, rate of change, thresholds and historical patterns are organized for analysis.

🤖

3. Predict

A machine-learning workflow estimates future water-level behaviour and risk signals.

🗺️

4. Explain

The dashboard turns complex signals into maps, forecasts, simple alerts and actions.
